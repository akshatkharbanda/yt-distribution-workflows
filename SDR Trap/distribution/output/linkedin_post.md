# LinkedIn Personal Post

Your first SDR is not a strategy.

It is a payroll line.

A founder raises money.

Then the first move feels obvious:

Hire SDRs.
Add a VP of Sales.
Buy tools.
Increase volume.

3 months later:

Calendar empty.
Burn rate vertical.
Team confused.
Market still does not care.

That is not scaling.

That is gambling with payroll.

Most founders copy the public part:

The SDRs.
The tools.
The VP title.
The revenue graph going up and to the right.

They do not copy the private process:

- failed messages
- bad targeting
- positioning experiments
- proof assets
- learning which pain gets a reply

The SDR is not the machine.

The SDR is the operator.

The machine is the outbound playbook:

- which pain creates curiosity
- which proof asset moves buyers
- which message does not get ignored

If the playbook is broken, hiring more reps just creates a bigger mess.

More volume will not save a dead message.

It just helps more people ignore you faster.
