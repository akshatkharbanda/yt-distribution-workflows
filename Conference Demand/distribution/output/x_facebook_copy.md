## X Thread Option

1/ Most B2B products have one uncomfortable problem:

The buyer may need it.

The product may be useful.

But nobody cares yet.

2/ That is why cold outbound by itself feels so hard.

You are not just fighting competitors.

You are fighting category indifference.

3/ Conferences change the context.

For 2 days, your buyers are in one building:

over-caffeinated, slightly underfed, and weirdly open to being pitched.

4/ But most companies waste it.

They buy a booth, stand there, collect a few conversations, and hope pipeline appears.

5/ The move is pre-event outbound.

Reach out before the event.
Book meetings before the event.
Give buyers a reason to notice you before the event.

6/ Cold outbound is not the standalone engine.

It is the multiplier.

## Facebook Copy

Most B2B products are useful.

But useful is not enough if the market has not paid attention yet.

That is the uncomfortable part of B2B demand.

Cold outbound by itself is hard.
Conferences by themselves are expensive and unpredictable.

But together, they can work.

Pre-event outbound gives buyers a reason to know you before they walk into the venue.

Then the conference becomes a rapid-fire discovery sequence, not an expensive networking field trip.
