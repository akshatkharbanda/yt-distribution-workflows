Short Version Under 900 Characters

Most B2B products do not have inherent demand. Normal people translation: Nobody cares yet. That is why cold outbound alone is so hard. But conferences change the context. For 2 days, your buyers are in one building: over-caffeinated, slightly underfed, and weirdly open to being pitched. Most companies waste this by buying a booth and hoping pipeline appears. The better move: reach out before the event, book meetings before the event, and give buyers a reason to notice you. Outbound is not always the engine. It is the multiplier.
