Full Founder-Essay Version

Most B2B products do not have inherent demand. Normal people translation: Nobody cares yet. That does not mean the product is bad. Maybe it is. Separate tragedy. But many useful B2B products still get ignored because buyers are not actively looking for the category. They are busy. Skeptical. Bombarded with pitches. Mildly curious at best. So "send more cold emails" is usually the wrong diagnosis. Cold outbound by itself is hard. Events by themselves are expensive and unpredictable. But together? Now outbound has a reason to exist. Because every buyer you want is suddenly trapped inside one building for 2 days. Highly over-caffeinated. Slightly underfed. Surprisingly willing to listen. This is where most companies waste the opportunity. They buy a booth. Stand there. Have a few decent conversations. Then hope pipeline magically appears. Meanwhile, ideal buyers walk past them and disappear forever. The move is pre-event outbound: Reach out before the event. Book meetings before the event. Give buyers a reason to notice you before the event. Then the conference becomes a rapid-fire discovery sequence. Not an expensive team getaway with a booth. Cold outbound is not the standalone engine. It is the multiplier. I broke this down in the full video. Watch the full video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iQdNmHKNYlc
