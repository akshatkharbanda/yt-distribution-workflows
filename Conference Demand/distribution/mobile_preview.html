LinkedIn Post
Most B2B products do not have inherent demand. Normal people translation: Nobody cares yet. That is why cold outbound alone is so hard. But conferences change the context. For 2 days, your buyers are in one building: over-caffeinated, slightly underfed, and weirdly open to being pitched. Most companies waste this by buying a booth and hoping pipeline appears. The better move: Reach out before the event. Book meetings before the event. Give buyers a reason to notice you before they walk past your booth. Outbound is not always the engine.
