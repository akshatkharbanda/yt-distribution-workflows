# Planned Script

Are you wondering...
why even people who NEED your B2B SaaS or service...
still ignore it?

Not because it sucks.

Well...
maybe it does.

But that is a separate tragedy.

The real problem is worse.

Most prospects are not actively looking for your category at all.

In economist language:

there is no inherent demand.

In normal people language:

"Nobody cares yet."

Now compare this with a new frontier AI model.

A new Chinese AI model launches.

It claims:

- smarter than OpenAI's latest model
- gigantic context window
- 10x cheaper

Suddenly everyone loses their mind.

People use the model for 5 minutes on OpenRouter...

then post about it for 15 hours.

Even though half the time people abandon these models by the second week.

But why?

Because people already understand the value of frontier AI.

Top-tier intelligence.

Lower cost.

Faster work.

Obvious upside.

OpenAI and Anthropic already created demand for the category.

Now everyone else is competing to capture it.

The market moves by itself.

That is what inherent demand looks like.

Most B2B companies like us do NOT get that luxury.

We have lower demand...

and intense competition.

Most B2B products are honestly...

useful.

Not magical.

Not revolutionary.

And that is okay.

Entire billion-dollar businesses are built on good products with strong distribution.

Because in B2B...

distribution is often equally important as product.

Especially when buyers are:

- busy
- skeptical
- bombarded with cold pitches
- and only mildly curious

So if your category does not have natural demand...

you need another strategy.

The companies winning pipeline consistently...

usually combine outbound...

with channels where buyers already pay attention.

Conferences.

Partnerships.

Founder content.

Cold outbound by itself is hard.

Events by themselves are unpredictable.

But together?

Now your outbound finally has a reason to exist.

Because every buyer you want...

is suddenly trapped in one building for two days.

Highly over-caffeinated.

Slightly underfed.

And surprisingly willing to listen to your pitch.

If done right...

conferences compress six months of LinkedIn outreach...

into one afternoon.

But most companies waste this completely.

They buy a booth.

Stand there awkwardly.

Get excited after a few decent conversations.

And hope pipeline magically appears.

Meanwhile their ideal buyer walks past them...

grabs a tote bag...

says "interesting idea"...

and disappears forever.

The move that quadruples your ROI on conferences...

is pre-event outbound.

Reach out BEFORE the event.

Book meetings BEFORE the event.

Give buyers a reason to notice you BEFORE the event.

Now the conference becomes a conversion mechanism.

Not an expensive networking field trip.

And something interesting happens.

You start looking much bigger than you actually are.

I am not even saying this.

My clients tell me this all the time.

Now let's talk partnerships.

Find companies already selling to your exact buyer.

Not competitors.

Adjacent players.

Agencies.

Consultants.

Implementation partners.

Tech partners.

Give them your product for free.

Let them get used to it.

Let them recommend it naturally.

Trust transfers.

One warm introduction from the right partner...

is worth more than thousands of random cold emails.

Not because outbound is dead.

But because outbound works much better...

when attached to trust.

And this is the part most companies misunderstand.

Cold outbound is not the standalone engine.

It is the multiplier.

It amplifies channels where attention already exists.

Outbound for conferences.

Outbound for partnerships.

Outbound for founder content.

Outbound for webinars.

Outbound for reports.

Now buyers feel like they keep seeing you everywhere.

And in B2B...

that feeling matters a lot.

Because buyers rarely purchase after one touchpoint.

They buy after repeated trust signals.

Repeated relevance.

Repeated exposure.

Now...

if you HAVE to do cold outbound...

pitch something easy to consume.

A 2-minute teaser demo.

A useful one-page PDF.

A short teardown.

A useful insight.

Not:

"Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call?"

For what exactly, Rohit?

Emotional damage?

And that sales asset...

should clearly communicate...

why your solution is worth $5k/month...

or $50k/month.

Now THAT is the hard part.

Anyway...

Most B2B companies do not fail because the product is terrible.

They fail because the market never paid enough attention.

Which is why the real game is not:

"Send more emails."

The real game is:

manufacture enough attention...

that buyers start conversations themselves.

And yes...

this is literally what my company BizAmps helps B2B companies build.

Conferences.

Partnerships.

Outbound.

Combined into one coordinated demand engine.
