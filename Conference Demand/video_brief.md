# Video Brief

## Title

The Biggest B2B Growth Hack Nobody Talks About On LinkedIn.

## Link

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iQdNmHKNYlc

## Core Idea

Most B2B products do not have inherent demand. Buyers are busy, skeptical, and not actively searching for the category.

The move is not simply "send more emails." The move is to attach outbound to channels where buyers already pay attention: conferences, partnerships, founder content, webinars, and reports.

For this video, the strongest example is conferences. Pre-event outbound gives cold outreach a reason to exist because the target buyers are already gathering in one place.

## Main Thesis

Cold outbound is not the standalone engine. It is the multiplier.

## Audience

B2B founders and revenue leaders, especially SaaS, IT services, AI services, founder-led sales teams, and companies trying to get more ROI from conferences and outbound.

## Key Ideas

- Most B2B products are useful, but not magical enough to create natural demand by themselves.
- The market often has not paid enough attention yet.
- Conferences by themselves are expensive and unpredictable.
- Cold outbound by itself is hard.
- Together, events and outbound can compress months of outreach into one afternoon.
- Most companies waste conferences by buying a booth and hoping conversations magically become pipeline.
- Pre-event outbound means reaching out, booking meetings, and giving buyers a reason to notice you before the event.
- Buyers rarely purchase after one touchpoint. They buy after repeated trust signals.
- BizAmps helps build coordinated demand engines across conferences, partnerships, and outbound.
