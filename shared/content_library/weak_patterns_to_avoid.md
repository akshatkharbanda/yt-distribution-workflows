# Weak Patterns To Avoid

Status: Initial rules; update after real campaign data.

## Avoid

- Generic AI marketing language.
- Corporate launch announcements.
- Long setup before the point.
- Fake urgency.
- Hype words like "game-changing", "revolutionary", "unlock", or "ultimate guide".
- Uncommon or clever words that slow the reader down.
- "Lanyards" in social posts unless the context really needs it; use "badges", "name tags", or "event badges".
- Phrases that sound written for marketers instead of founders.
- Treating weak replies as strong intent.
- Making every email sound like a summary instead of a reason to watch.
- Creating every channel by default.
- Creating 5-10 variants when 2-3 strong messages would be better.
- Copy that sounds like generic repurposed content instead of a personal message from Akshat.
- Newsletter-style WhatsApp messages.
- Over-polished copy that loses founder directness.
- Cute phrases that sound clever but weak.
- "Calendar invite with invoices."

## Needs Data

- Which hooks get opens but no clicks.
- Which topics get views but no replies.
- Which channels create noise but no useful demand.
