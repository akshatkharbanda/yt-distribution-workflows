# Octolens Keyword Plan

## Rule

Never auto-reply.

Octolens can surface opportunities. Akshat approves any reply or follow-up.

## Starting Keyword Groups

### BizAmps Core

- outbound
- B2B lead generation
- demand generation
- cold outreach
- sales follow-up
- CRM reactivation
- HubSpot automation
- Clay enrichment
- Smartlead

### YouTube Topic Demand

- founder-led sales
- SDR hiring
- outbound playbook
- message-market fit
- event outreach
- conference demand
- sales pipeline
- GTM systems

### Problem Signals

- no replies
- low meetings
- cold email not working
- SDR not booking meetings
- HubSpot follow-up
- stale leads
- event leads not converting

## Junk-Alert Detection

Flag as junk when:

- The mention is generic tool spam.
- The post is hiring-only with no business problem.
- The keyword appears in an unrelated context.
- The account looks like an agency scraping engagement.
- The comment asks for free tools only and has no GTM context.
- The signal is just "thanks", "sure", "okay", or "send details".

## Weekly Keyword Cleanup

Every week:

- Remove keywords creating mostly junk.
- Split broad keywords into narrower intent phrases.
- Add exact phrases from real comments, replies, and clicked campaigns.
- Keep a short "watchlist" instead of tracking everything.

## Reply Opportunity Scoring

Score manually from 0 to 5:

- 5: founder/revenue leader has a clear GTM pain and asks for help or examples.
- 4: strong pain signal, relevant company, no direct ask yet.
- 3: relevant discussion, possible learning/comment opportunity.
- 2: weak or generic mention.
- 1: mostly junk.
- 0: irrelevant or unsafe.

Only scores 4-5 should become reply candidates.

## Future Video Idea Scoring

Score topics from 0 to 5:

- 5: repeated pain across comments, campaigns, and conversations.
- 4: strong pain from one channel plus clear BizAmps point of view.
- 3: useful topic but demand is still thin.
- 2: interesting but not obviously useful for buyers.
- 1: too generic.
- 0: off-brand.

## Weekly Output

- Top useful mentions.
- Junk keywords to remove.
- Keywords to add.
- Reply candidates for Akshat review.
- Future video ideas with evidence.
